Privacy Policy

隐私政策 · English first, 中文在后

Summary

Blueee does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics or advertising SDKs. All data lives on your iPhone.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Location (including “Always” if you allow it): to record where you were when a device connects or disconnects, and to keep a 24-hour trail. Stored locally in the app database; older points are removed automatically.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to devices you add, detect connect/disconnect in the background and read signal strength. Device names and identifiers are stored locally.
  • Camera (“Walk to Locate”): ARKit tracks the route your phone walks. Images are processed live and are never photographed, saved or uploaded.
  • Motion (AirPods head tracking): read live to steer the audio cue; never saved or uploaded.

Sharing and retention

None of this data leaves your device, so we cannot access, share or sell it. Deleting the app deletes its data. You can revoke any permission in iOS Settings → Blueee.

Children

Blueee does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: open an issue at github.com/whlan02/blueee/issues.

隐私政策（中文）

Blueee 不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。没有账号、没有服务器，也没有统计或广告 SDK。所有数据只保存在你的 iPhone 上。

  • 位置（如你允许“始终”）：用于记录设备连上或断开时你所在的位置，并保留 24 小时轨迹。仅存本机，过期自动清除。
  • 蓝牙：用于连接你添加的设备、在后台感知连上/断开、读取信号强度。设备名称和标识仅存本机。
  • 相机（“走一走定位”）：ARKit 跟踪手机走过的路线，画面实时处理，不拍照、不保存、不上传。
  • 运动（AirPods 头部追踪）：实时读取用于调整声音方向，不保存、不上传。

这些数据不会离开你的设备，因此我们无法访问、分享或出售。删除 App 即删除全部数据；你可随时在 iOS 设置 → Blueee 中撤销权限。Blueee 不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的数据。联系方式：GitHub Issues。
