FAQ

Why does it ask for “Always” location?
So it can record where you were the moment a device disconnects while the app is in the background. It still works with “While Using”, but may miss background disconnects.

Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

A device is not detected in the background.
Make sure Bluetooth is on, Background App Refresh is allowed, and the app wasn’t force-quit from the app switcher.

为什么要求“始终”位置？
为了在 App 处于后台时，设备断开的瞬间也能记下你的位置。选“使用期间”也能用，但可能漏掉后台断开。

数据会上传吗？
不会，全部保存在你的 iPhone 上。
