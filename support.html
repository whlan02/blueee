FAQ

Why does it ask for “Always” location?
So it can record where you were the moment a device disconnects while the app is in the background. “While Using” also works but may miss background disconnects.

Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

A device is not detected in the background.
Make sure Bluetooth is on, Background App Refresh is allowed, and the app was not force-quit from the app switcher.
